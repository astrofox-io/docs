Astrofox keeps project structure and common actions on the left, the rendered
stage in the center, element controls on the right, and audio playback along
the bottom. Selecting an item in one area updates the others, so you can move
between arranging, previewing, and fine-tuning without changing views.

## Workspace map

| Area | Location | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [App bar and actions](/docs/interface/app-bar-and-actions) | Top and far left | Opens, saves, and exports projects; opens settings; shows or hides panels |
| [Layers panel](/docs/interface/layers-panel) | Upper left | Organizes scenes, displays, and effects |
| [Reactors panel](/docs/interface/reactors-panel) | Lower left | Creates signals that can drive compatible controls |
| [Stage](/docs/interface/stage) | Center | Previews the composition and provides direct transform and camera tools |
| [Controls panel](/docs/interface/controls-panel) | Right | Edits the selected scene, display, or effect |
| [Player and status](/docs/interface/player-and-status) | Bottom | Plays audio, selects live input, changes stage zoom, and reports app status |

## How the areas work together

The **Layers** and **Controls** panels are selection-linked. Select a scene,
display, or effect on the left and its control card becomes active on the
right. When the Controls panel is showing every layer, selecting a layer also
scrolls its card into view.

The **Reactors** list has its own selection. Selecting a reactor opens its
detailed signal editor above the player. You can then bind that signal from a
compatible numeric control in the Controls panel.

The **Stage** reflects both kinds of changes immediately. Layer edits change
what is drawn; reactor and player changes update how the composition moves.

## A practical editing loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the structure">
    Add scenes, displays, and effects in the Layers panel, then drag them into
    the order you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the look">
    Select a layer and change its values in Controls. Use the Stage transform
    tool when direct manipulation is faster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add motion">
    Load audio or enable live input, create a reactor, and bind it to controls
    such as scale, rotation, opacity, or effect strength.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and refine">
    Play or scrub the track while watching the stage. Tune the reactor and
    control range until the response matches the sound.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make more room

Use the three panel buttons in the app bar to show or hide the left, bottom,
or right areas. This changes only the workspace layout; it does not remove
layers, change the canvas size, or affect an export.

<Tip>
  Hover an icon when its purpose is unclear. Action, stage, player, and panel
  buttons expose labels in tooltips.
</Tip>
